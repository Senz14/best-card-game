EMBERFALL DUEL

A card duel over the last ember of a fallen world.

You face The Ashen Warden. Each turn your Ember grows by one. Spend it on creatures and spells, trade on the table, and burn the Warden from 20 health to 0 before your own flame dies. Guard creatures must be attacked first; Spark effects fire the moment a creature lands. Click a card to play it, click one of your ready creatures then a target to attack, and press Enter or the End Turn button to pass.
